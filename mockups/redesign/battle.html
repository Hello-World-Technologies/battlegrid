Battle screen redesign

  • 1Top bar. Chunky pause button, level name and a timer with the three star goals. The stars go dark as the 3-star and 2-star times pass, so speed is visible while you play.
  • 2Power bar with your face. The mascot marks your share and a legend names each rival colour with its percentage. Today the bar is unlabelled.
  • 33D troops. The equipped troop skin (here the Warplane) flies on a disc in the owner colour, so you can tell whose squad it is.
  • 4"Takes it" feedback. While dragging, a floating chip shows how many troops you are sending and the target ring turns green when you can win.
  • 5Item dock. Replaces the single bomb button: your held items in one row, with counts and the "uses left" pips. Picking one raises it and shows a gold prompt on top.
  • 6Mascot tips. The tips the game already generates (factory, fortress, storm, outnumbered) are spoken by the water sprite instead of being unseen.

Style: the logo's blue and gold, thick navy outlines with a white rim, Lilita One. This is a design proposal; the battlefield itself is still drawn by Skia.
